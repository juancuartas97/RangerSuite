Suggested flow improvements

Use these patterns across redesign pages to lower time-to-insight and reduce form drop-off.

1) Report creation flow (guided)

  • Start with specialty + survey selection, then save defaults for future reports.
  • Move into stepped input forms and autosave after each section.
  • Show live percentile feedback during entry to prevent rework at the end.
  • Finish with a review checkpoint highlighting missing/atypical fields before export.

2) Benchmark exploration flow

  • Open with global search + suggested recent terms rather than deep category scrolling.
  • Persist active filter chips at top so users always know why they see current results.
  • Allow quick compare from the table into a side-by-side tray.
  • Enable "save view" and "share view" to support recurring contract prep workflows.

3) Analytics decision flow

  • Lead with top KPIs and trend delta badges for fast weekly reviews.
  • Prioritize exceptions first: over-benchmark spend, contract expirations, low sample confidence.
  • Link each analytic row directly to relevant contracts and documentation actions.
  • Offer one-click export to briefing PDFs for stakeholders.
These flows are designed to stay visually aligned with your provided examples while modernizing interaction patterns.
