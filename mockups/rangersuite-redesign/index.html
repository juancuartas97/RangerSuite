Design principles used

  • Preserve familiar left navigation and palette for continuity with existing users.
  • Increase scannability through card hierarchy and tighter typography rhythm.
  • Reduce cognitive load by grouping actions and exposing only relevant controls per step.
  • Use contextual hints (percentile badges, progress indicators, warnings) near user inputs.
